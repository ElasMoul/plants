Foundations

Colors

Colour in your garden does two jobs and no others: it says what kind of thing a card is, and it says what state that thing is in. Everything else on screen is chalk on clay — and the whole key can be re-cut live, from Settings, while you are standing in the world.

What this is

Your garden is drawn as one world of cards. A card can be a species, a plant you actually own, a problem, a guide, a journal entry, a room in the house. At the scale where its name is unreadable you still need to know which of those you are looking at, and whether it is thriving or in trouble. That is what this page settles: one colour per node kind, one colour per health and confidence reading, one colour per degradation state, and a chalk-white structural voice that means nothing at all — so the coloured marks are free to mean something.

Colour lives in three layers, and reference runs one way only. Layer 1 primitives (--rz-leaf-300) are raw and mean nothing. Layer 2 semantics (--rz-kind-plant, --rz-ailing) are what a raw value means, and layer 2 is restated in full by every theme and every palette. Layer 3 is this system's own geometry and density, which a palette never touches. A page writes layer 2. It never writes a hex, an rgba(), or a layer-1 step.

Two families listed on this page are owned elsewhere. The chrome family (--rz-chrome-*) belongs to chrome, and the mutation voice (--rz-mutate) belongs to action rail and interaction and cursors. They appear in the tables so you can find their values; their laws are not restated here.

The laws

Law 1 · C12

Kind is a channel. Every domain node kind has exactly one colour token, that token is spent on the kind graduation and nowhere else, and so a card answers “what kind of thing is this” at a size where its name is unreadable.

In the world below, hop until a card of each kind has been drawn at rank fringe. Apply a 6px blur to .rz-plane and screenshot: the graduations are still separable by colour. Then, for each --rz-kind-* token, assert the only computed property in the document resolving to it is background on the .rz-n__grad of cards carrying that data-kind-key.

Law 2 · C23, C24

Colour never carries a meaning alone. Every health reading, every confidence grade and every degradation state also states itself in the card's own words, inside that same card.

Apply grayscale(1) to the whole document. Every health dot still sits beside its word, every confidence bar beside HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW, and an unfetched card still reads differently from an empty one from its text alone. Nothing on screen has lost a meaning.

Law 3 · C9

Palettes switch live. One attribute on the root re-keys every colour on screen — cards, veins, chrome and the drifting field alike — with no save step, and nothing moves.

Record every card's computed left/top and RZ.extent(), call RZ.setPalette("terrarium"), and diff: both unchanged. Read --rz-mote off the root before and after: it changed, and the canvas repainted with the new value rather than its start-up copy.

Law 4 · C12

Every colour on screen is a token reference. A page writes layer 2 only; a raw value anywhere outside assets/tokens.css is a defect, not a shortcut.

Scan every page and every stylesheet other than tokens.css for #, rgb( or rgba(. The only permitted matches are doc-swatch backgrounds, and every one of those is a var(--rz-…). Then switch palette: any mark that fails to re-key was a literal.

Specimens

The kind channel, in the world

Eight kinds on one plane, drawn by the system from its own edge list. Click a card to travel to it; the ranks recompute and the graduations grow and shrink, but a kind's colour never changes. Blur your eyes: the names go first, the colour stays.

Two readings are living inside those cards. The Office Fig carries a health dot with its word beside it; the leaf-spot card carries a confidence bar with its grade beside it, and a sentence that hedges in plain words. Travel into either to see them in full — and note that “Further out” is drawn as unfetched, not as empty, in border, wash and wording, not only in hue.

Switch the key while you read

Press one. The whole page re-keys — this article, the sidebar, the cards above and the drifting field, because the canvas reads its colours back out of the same tokens. Nothing is saved, and nothing moves.

Both specimens are wired by the system, not by this page:

<button type="button" class="rz-palette"
        data-rz-palette="terrarium" aria-pressed="false">…</button>

RZ.mount({
  edges: [["n-garden", "n-study"], ["n-garden", "n-ficus"]],
  focus: "n-garden"
});
RZ.setPalette("terrarium");   /* exactly what Settings calls */

Tokens

Kind — one colour per domain node kind

Eight kinds, eight tokens, and a theme fills all eight or none. Spend a kind colour on anything but the graduation and Law 1's check fails on its second assertion.

Kind channel
TokenMeans
--rz-kind-collection A grouping you keep — a room, a shelf, a windowsill full of pots.
--rz-kind-species A species in general, not a plant of yours. Pale leaf: living, but not in your care.
--rz-kind-plant A plant you actually own, with a nickname. The fullest leaf on screen.
--rz-kind-guide Care instructions — window light rather than tissue, because a guide is not alive.
--rz-kind-problem Something wrong: a pest, a disease, a deficiency. Lesion colour.
--rz-kind-journal Something you wrote down on a day. Pencil on a care tag.
--rz-kind-platform PlantPal itself — your garden as a whole, and the places the app speaks from.
--rz-kind-region A part of the world not fetched yet. Dim on purpose: it is real, it is just not here yet.

The structural voices

Everything that is not kind and not state is chalk. Chalk means nothing, which is what leaves the coloured marks free to mean something. Clay is the single exception, and it belongs to mutation alone.

What a palette may move

Palette scope
LayerA palette mayWhy
Layer 1 — primitivesNever touch them.They are raw values shared by every key. Moving one moves five meanings at once.
Layer 2 — semanticsRestate any of them, declaring only what it moves.This is the key. Whatever it leaves alone inherits from the default.
Layer 3 — systemNever touch it.Density, the ladder and the lattice are not a matter of key. A palette changes colour, never geometry — that is Law 3's check.

Accessibility

Colour is never the only channel. This is Law 2, and it is the one to check first. A health dot always sits beside its word. A confidence bar always sits beside HIGH, MEDIUM or LOW, and a low-confidence answer hedges in a plain sentence as well. An unfetched card and an empty card differ in border, in wash and in wording, not only in hue — which is what keeps them apart for a reader who cannot separate --rz-unknown from --rz-empty.

The pairing that needs watching. Leaf against lesion — --rz-thriving against --rz-ailing — is the pairing this key leans on hardest, and it is exactly the pairing red-green colour blindness collapses. That is why neither is ever asked to carry a reading by itself, in any palette. Test by applying grayscale(1) to the running world: every state must still read.

Contrast, and who owns it. --rz-ink and --rz-ink-second clear 4.5:1 against every surface token in every palette, so prose is safe anywhere. --rz-ink-muted is for captions and incidental lines, not for a sentence a reader must follow. A palette that restates an ink token owns that contrast: check it against --rz-void, --rz-sheet and --rz-chrome-fill before you ship it. Kind colours are never asked to carry text — a graduation is a 2px rule — so they answer to separability from each other, not to a text contrast ratio.

The focus ring is not the focused card. --rz-focus sits deliberately outside the chalk family: keyboard focus and the focused node are two different things, and a reader tabbing through must be able to tell which is which. It is applied by the base layer to every focusable element. Never remove an outline, and never restyle it per component.

Switching palette announces itself. Every palette control is a real button carrying aria-pressed, and the system says “Palette applied. Nothing was saved and nothing moved.” into the live region — because a change a sighted reader takes in at a glance is otherwise completely silent. Colour states nothing to a screen reader; the words beside it do all the work.

Do and don't

Do

Let kind colour say kind and health colour say health, each with its word beside it: a leaf-coloured graduation on a plant card, and “Keep an eye on it” next to the amber dot.

Keep an eye on it

Don’t

Spend a health colour on the graduation to say “this plant is struggling”. Two channels now claim the same 2px rule, the kind reading is gone at fringe scale, and Law 1's check fails.

Do

Write the meaning: color: var(--rz-ailing). It re-keys in every palette and both themes without you thinking about it.

Don’t

Write var(--rz-necrosis-300) because it happens to be today's value. You have pinned one palette's reading into a page, and Law 4's scan will find it.

Do

Add a colour by adding a semantic token under a named amendment, recorded in the constitution's register, and say so in one sentence on your page.

Don’t

Re-key an existing semantic token to get the shade you want. Pages already written against it change meaning silently — see extending this system.
