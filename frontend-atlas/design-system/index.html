Rhizome

A rhizome is the underground stem a plant runs sideways — no trunk, no crown, just a live network where any point can put up a shoot. That is the shape of PlantPal's data, and it is the shape of its interface: one plane, nodes on a lattice, veins between them, and a camera that travels.

What this system settles

PlantPal is a warm, plain-spoken plant companion — the knowledgeable friend, not the botanist and not the dashboard. Rhizome is the world that companion lives in. Your garden is a place you enter: a plant, a species, a problem, a treatment course, a journal entry and an unfetched region are all nodes on the same plane, joined by veins that are drawn before anyone travels them.

Rhizome is atlas-class. It is judged by the atlas design law, C1–C26, and by no other regime's law. That register is a file, not a memory: constitution.json carries all twenty-six invariants written out in full, each with the check that settles it, alongside the numbers every page and every theme reads — the density threshold, the navigation timing, the lattice pitch, the zoom clamp, the slow threshold. Beside it sit the three specs the doctrine has always required and which now ship with the system: layout-determinism, traversal-map and degradation-spec.

A theme round chooses a key, a type voice and a density. Everything else — how the world works — is settled here. If you find yourself deciding how traversal, chrome, rank, density or degradation behaves, you are reading the wrong file: read the authoring contract and then the page that owns the law.

The principles

Six, each an invariant with a check. A principle you cannot check is a preference, and a preference is lost by the next round. They are set out in full in PRINCIPLES.md.

1 · The world is the interface C1, C2, C4

There is one plane and everything is on it. Nothing is mounted, unmounted, routed to or replaced; going somewhere is the camera rescaling that plane, so the place you left is still in sight when you arrive.

After ten hops the element set on the plane is identical, the previous focus is still rendered, and history.length has not moved.

2 · Geography is a promise C7, C8, C9

A node's position is a formula — origin + cell × pitch + the user's own persisted offset — stable for the life of the record. Only a user drag and a genuinely new node ever change it.

Load twice and diff the computed left/top of every node: equal. Insert a node: equal. Toggle every degraded state: equal.

3 · Register before decoration C14, C16, C17

Chrome and content are different materials, not different layers. Chrome is flush to a viewport edge, never moves with the camera, takes no shadow at all, and speaks in tracked uppercase monospace at one rung. A node floats on the plane and travels with it.

Blur the screen: the cards are the only bright soft rectangles on it.

4 · Scale is the argument C3, C12

Rank is computed — graph distance from the focus, recomputed every hop — and spent on size: four card widths, stepped ink, per-rank height caps, and a graduation on the card's head whose length is the rank and whose colour is the kind.

Freeze a frame, blur the text, and the hierarchy still reads.

5 · Every motion states which edge was taken C10, C11, C13, C21

One navigation timing — 300ms — for every hop from every trigger, and one code path that performs it. The camera travels along the real edges by arc length, so the motion itself says which vein was crossed.

Sample the camera centre each frame and assert every sample lies on the route's polyline.

6 · Say what is true, per node C22, C23, C24, C25

Slowness, staleness, absence and failure are drawn inside the node they belong to, in that node's own words — never a global banner, never a spinner, never a modal. Unknown and empty are different things and are drawn differently.

Walk every state from the probe panel without editing markup, and diff the geography each time.

The world, running

Five nodes, four veins, one camera. Click a card to travel to it — the camera follows the vein rather than cutting across the space, the rank of every card is recomputed from where you now are, and nothing on the page is replaced.
